## How this doc is updated

The track-items loop updates this doc every Sunday after the 10:00 brief. Only you can see it, and Claude never changes its sharing.

- **Chart:** Claude updates the current month every week. A month stops changing when it ends.
- **Weekly log:** Claude adds an entry for the previous work week (Sunday to Thursday, plus weekend work).
- **Monthly summaries:** Claude adds a summary when a month ends.
- **Highlights:** Claude adds a win when you say "log this win" in a chat, or when a week has a clear win.
- **PRs merged:** your PRs that merged in the period (GitHub).
- **PRs reviewed:** in the chart, PRs by other people that were opened in that month and that you reviewed. In the weekly log, PRs that you submitted a review on in that week.
- **Issues done:** your Linear issues that moved to a completed state.
